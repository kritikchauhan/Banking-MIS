# Loan Portfolio MIS & Risk Analytics Dashboard (Excel)

## Introduction
This project is an Excel-based MIS and risk analytics dashboard built to evaluate a retail loan portfolio from a financial services perspective.  
It focuses on portfolio health, customer segmentation, credit risk indicators, and operational compliance metrics commonly tracked by banks and NBFCs for management reporting and regulatory oversight.

## Objectives
- Assess loan portfolio composition across demographic and product dimensions  
- Monitor credit risk through default and loan grade indicators  
- Evaluate operational compliance via verification status  
- Identify concentration risk and data quality gaps impacting underwriting decisions  
- Deliver MIS-ready insights aligned with lending and risk management practices  

## Key Metrics
- **Total Loans Disbursed:** Overall portfolio size  
- **Loan Volume by Age Group:** Customer segmentation for acquisition strategy  
- **Product-wise Loan Count:** Exposure by loan product (IML, XL, etc.)  
- **Default Loan Count:** Measure of portfolio stress  
- **Default Rate (%):** Core credit risk indicator  
- **Verified vs Unverified Loans:** Operational and KYC compliance metric  
- **Loan Grade Distribution:** Credit quality and underwriting effectiveness  

## KPIs
- **Default Rate (1.56%):** Indicates strong credit control and conservative risk exposure  
- **Verification Rate:** Measures adherence to KYC and internal policy standards  
- **Youth Portfolio Share (18–25):** Growth-focused but risk-sensitive segment  
- **Product Concentration Ratio:** Identifies reliance on specific loan products  
- **Unknown Grade Percentage:** Data quality and credit assessment gap indicator  

## Key Features
- Portfolio-level MIS dashboard aligned with banking reporting standards  
- Demographic risk profiling (age and religion-wise exposure)  
- Product performance analysis to assess revenue and exposure drivers  
- Credit risk visibility using default and grade classification  
- Compliance monitoring through verification status tracking  
- Interactive filters enabling management-level drill-down analysis  

## Insights
- **Youth-led Growth:** The 18–25 age group accounts for 23K+ loans, highlighting aggressive penetration in early-credit lifecycle customers, which requires tighter monitoring despite current low defaults.  
- **Controlled Credit Risk:** A low default rate of 1.56% reflects effective underwriting and collections strategy at the current portfolio stage.  
- **Credit Grading Gaps:** A majority of loans classified as *“Unknown”* grade weakens risk segmentation and limits accurate PD (Probability of Default) assessment.  
- **Product Dependency:** IML and XL products dominate the portfolio, indicating potential concentration risk if macro or policy conditions impact these segments.  
- **Demographic Concentration:** Hindu borrowers represent ~75% of loans, reflecting demographic skew that should be reviewed against geographic and market expansion goals.  
- **Operational Risk Exposure:** 16.9K+ unverified loans signal KYC and compliance lapses that could lead to regulatory and reputational risk if unaddressed.  

## Technology Used
- Microsoft Excel  
- Pivot Tables for multidimensional portfolio analysis  
- Pivot Charts for MIS visualization  
- Advanced Excel formulas (SUMIFS, IF, VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP)  
- Slicers for dynamic portfolio filtering  

## Conclusion
This dashboard demonstrates practical domain understanding of retail lending, credit risk management, and MIS reporting.  
It effectively bridges raw transaction data with business-focused insights required by banks, NBFCs, and financial analytics teams, making it a strong portfolio project for MIS Executive, Data Analyst, and Finance Analyst roles.
